A developer who believes everyone deserves access to quality coding education — no paywalls, no gatekeeping.

I started splatbasset.dev because I remember how overwhelming it felt to learn programming for the first time. Too many resources assumed you already knew things. I want to change that.

Whether you want to build websites, create games, develop apps, or just understand how software works — I've got you covered. My courses focus on practical, project-based learning because the best way to learn is by doing.

Currently, I'm building courses for Java, HTML, CSS, and C# — four languages that open up a world of possibilities. More languages and topics are on the way.

What Drives Me

Passion for Teaching

Seeing someone go from confused to confident is the most rewarding thing. That's why I do this.

Real-World Skills

No toy examples. Everything I teach connects to skills that matter in real development jobs and projects.

Always Evolving
